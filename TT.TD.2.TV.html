TT.TD.PP.2.TVR Documentation

calculate virtual temperature from ambient and dew point temperature

Description

TT.TD.PP.2.RH calculates the virtual temperature of an air parcel from its ambient temperature (K or °C) and dew point temperature (K or °C)

Usage

TT.TD.PP.2.TV(tt, td, pp=1013.25)

Arguments

tt

ambient temperature (K >150 or °C<150)

td

dew point temperature (K >150 or °C<150)

pp

ambient pressure (hPa)

Details

TT.TD.PP.2.TV calculates the virtual temperature of an air parcel from its ambient temperature (K or °C) and dew point temperature (K or °C).

Value

virtual temperature in K
