TT.TD.2.RHR Documentation

calculate relative humidity from ambient and dew point temperature

Description

TT.TD.2.RH calculates the relative humidity (%) of an air parcel from its ambient temperature (K or °C) and dew point temperature (K or °C)

Usage

TT.TD.2.RH(tt, td)

Arguments

tt

ambient temperature (K >150 or °C<150)

td

dew point temperature (K >150 or °C<150)

Details

TT.TD.2.RH calculates the relative humidity (%) of an air parcel from its ambient temperature (K or °C) and dew point temperature (K or °C).

Value

relative humidity (%)

Author(s)

Stehpan Henne

Examples

    print(TT.TD.2.RH(20, 20))
    print(TT.TD.2.RH(20, 10))
