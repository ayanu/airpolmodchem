TT.SH.2.TVR Documentation

calculate virtual temperature from temperature and specific humidity

Description

TT.SH.2.TV calculates the virtual temperature (K) of an air parcel from its ambient temperature (K or °C) and specific humidity (kg/kg)

Usage

TT.SH.2.TV(tt, sh, pp=1013.25)

Arguments

tt

ambient temperature (K >150 or °C<150)

sh

specific humidity (kg/kg)

pp

ambient pressure (hPa)

Details

TT.SH.2.TV calculates the virtual temperature (K) of an air parcel from its ambient temperature (K or °C) and specific humidity (kg/kg)

Value

virtual temperature in units (K)
