# Calculate water vapor partial pressure of an air parcel from specific humidity

Calculate water vapor partial pressure of an air parcel given in (hPa)
from the air parcel's specific humidity (kg/kg) and its ambient pressure
(hPa).

## Usage

``` r
SH.PP.2.E(sh, pp=1013.25)
```

## Arguments

- sh:

  specific humidity (kg/kg)

- pp:

  ambient pressure (hPa)

## Value

water vapor partial pressure (hPa)
